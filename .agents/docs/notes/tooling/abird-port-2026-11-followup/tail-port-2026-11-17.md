### Known flake (pre-existing, not caused by this port)

`fleet::build_backend::agent::acquisition::native::service::tests::descriptor_transfer_preserves_original_exclusion`
(`src/fleet/build_backend/agent/acquisition/native/service.rs:412`) fails
intermittently — reproduced on unmodified upstream bytes. It receives a flock fd
over `SCM_RIGHTS` (no `FD_CLOEXEC`) and asserts release after `drop`; a
concurrently forked test child can inherit the fd and hold the lock. Observed
once in a lint run and once in a plain local `cargo test` run; passes on rerun.
